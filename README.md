## 👩‍💻 About Me

I'm a **Software Engineering graduate** with a strong background in network infrastructure, currently transitioning into **Post-Quantum Cryptography (PQC)** and **Cybersecurity research**. My work sits at the intersection of theoretical cryptography and real-world systems — from satellite communications to IoT security.

I'm actively applying for **MSc programs in Cybersecurity and Cryptography**, driven by the belief that quantum-resistant systems will define the next era of secure communications.

> *"The transition to post-quantum security isn't optional — it's inevitable. I want to help build it."*

## 🔬 Research

### 📄 Published Paper
**Post-Quantum Cryptography for the Internet of Things: Challenges, Solutions, and Future Directions**
> Explores the vulnerabilities of IoT infrastructure to quantum threats and proposes PQC-based mitigation strategies, analyzing the trade-offs between security, performance, and resource constraints in embedded environments.

---

### 🎓 Bachelor's Thesis
**Post-Quantum and Quantum Key Distribution for Satellite Communications: Threats, Architectures, and Future Directions**
> Investigates hybrid QKD–PQC architectures for securing satellite communication channels against both classical and quantum adversaries. Covers threat modeling, protocol design, and forward-looking deployment challenges in space-based networks.
