## About

Software Engineering graduate moving from network infrastructure into **post-quantum cryptography and cybersecurity research**. My work bridges theoretical cryptographic design and deployment-level constraints — quantum key distribution for satellite links, and post-quantum schemes for resource-limited IoT devices. Currently seeking MSc opportunities in Cybersecurity and Cryptography.

## Research

**Published Paper** — *Post-Quantum Cryptography for the Internet of Things: Challenges, Solutions, and Future Directions*
Examines IoT infrastructure's exposure to quantum threats and evaluates PQC-based mitigations under real embedded-system constraints (security, performance, resource footprint).

**Bachelor's Thesis** — *Post-Quantum and Quantum Key Distribution for Satellite Communications: Threats, Architectures, and Future Directions*
Proposes hybrid QKD–PQC architectures for satellite links, covering threat modeling against classical and quantum adversaries, protocol design, and deployment challenges in space-based networks.

**Research interests:** Post-Quantum Cryptography (Kyber, Dilithium, SPHINCS+) · Quantum Key Distribution · Satellite & Space Communication Security · IoT & Lightweight Cryptography · Secure Protocol Design
